The short version

The AI Disciple offers seven services: AI opportunity assessment, workflow design and build, team training and adoption, an ongoing support retainer, website and software modernization, ministry and nonprofit media production, and AI social media marketing with personal brand clones.

Most engagements begin with the assessment and grow from there. Every one starts with a free 20-minute call and an honest answer about whether the work is worth your money. Pricing is quoted per engagement — ask on the call and you will get a realistic range on the spot.

Service 01

AI Opportunity Assessment

A structured working session with you and the people who actually do the work. We map how the week is really spent — not how the org chart says it is spent — and identify where AI can carry weight.

You leave with a written shortlist of the three to five workflows worth building, each ranked by hours saved, difficulty, and risk. That document is yours whether or not you hire me for anything else.

  • Time audit. Where the repetitive hours are hiding across roles.
  • Ranked shortlist. Three to five candidate workflows with estimated hours saved.
  • Risk and data review. What should never go near a language model, and why.
  • Tooling recommendation. Usually fewer tools than you were expecting.

Service 02

Workflow Design & Build

I build the actual working process, not a list of suggestions. That means the prompts, the templates, the input format, the place the output lands, the person who owns it, and the check that catches errors.

  • Tested, documented prompt sets tuned to your material and your voice
  • Templates and input forms so results are repeatable, not lucky
  • Verification steps — a way to check output against a real source
  • Written run-books your staff can follow without calling me
  • Automation and hand-off between tools where it pays for itself

Service 03

Team Training & Adoption

Hands-on sessions using your real work, not generic examples. Adoption is where most AI projects quietly die, so training is treated as part of the build rather than an optional extra.

  • Half-day introductory workshop for the whole team
  • Role-specific sessions for the people who will use it daily
  • Follow-up clinics a few weeks later, once the real questions surface
  • A short, readable AI usage policy your staff will actually follow
  • Plain-language guidance on verifying output and spotting confident errors

Service 04

Ongoing Support Retainer

A monthly arrangement for organizations that want somebody to call when a tool changes, a workflow breaks, or a new idea needs a sanity check before anyone spends money on it.

  • A set block of hours each month, used however you need them
  • Priority response — within one business day
  • Quarterly review of what is working and what should be retired
  • New workflows built as the need appears
  • Meaningful discount on agreements of six months or longer

Service 05

Website & Software Modernization

Thirty-five years of professional development experience, available when the AI plan collides with an aging system that has to be fixed first.

  • Website rebuilds, redesigns and performance work
  • Making an existing site readable to search engines and AI assistants
  • Custom development in C#, ASP.NET, Angular and modern web stacks
  • Integrating AI into software you already own
  • Straight technical advice before you buy something you do not need

Service 06

Church, Ministry & Nonprofit Video

Scripted, narrated, captioned video produced with an AI pipeline — 471 videos published in the last nine months, in English and Urdu, with Swahili work produced for partners in Kenya.

For churches that mostly means narrated Scripture films: a witness tells the story — Moses recounting creation, John describing Patmos — while the visuals carry the scene, built as a visual aid behind your message rather than a replacement for it. Also multi-week teaching series, children's Bible cartoons with printable worksheets, archaeology and background explainers, and testimony or appeal films for nonprofits.

Same production discipline as the client workflows, applied to media: research, script you approve before anything is produced, narration, visuals, captions, thumbnails, and publishing to YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. I will produce your material or teach your own volunteers to do it. Churches getting started also get free training, a free consultation and the free children's library regardless of budget.

Service 07

AI Social Media & Personal Brand Clones

Two problems here, and they usually belong to the same person. Owners who know they should be visible online and never post, because filming yourself eats an hour once you have set up, lost your place and started over. And owners who do post, faithfully, into a platform that was never going to reach their customers.

The clone. I build an AI presenter trained on your face and your voice, so material goes out with you in it without you being on camera. You approve the script, the clone delivers it, and a week of content stops costing a week. It also speaks languages you do not — I publish in English and Urdu as a matter of routine, which matters when your customers or your congregation do not all share one.

The strategy. A clone aimed at the wrong audience is just faster failure. So the other half of this is choosing the two platforms worth your time and ignoring the rest, working out which format actually travels on them, and setting a rhythm you can hold without it quietly becoming somebody's second job. Then the production line to feed it: hooks, captions, thumbnails, tags and scheduling.

The proof is the pipeline already running: 471 videos written, produced and published in nine months across YouTube, TikTok and Instagram, in two languages, by one person. That is the same machinery pointed at your business instead of mine.

One rule I do not bend. I clone a person who has agreed to it, in their own voice, and I will tell you plainly where the disclosure belongs. Cloning somebody who has not agreed — a competitor, a public figure, a relative who has died — is not work I will take.

Who this is for

Organizations I am built to help — and ones I am not

A good fit

  • Small and medium organizations, roughly 2 to 200 people
  • No IT department, no appetite for a large consulting firm
  • Independent businesses and professional practices
  • Churches, ministries and faith-based nonprofits
  • Schools, community organizations and bilingual congregations
  • Anyone whose staff are drowning in repetitive paperwork

Not a good fit

  • Organizations looking to cut headcount — that is not the work I do
  • Anyone who wants AI to make legally binding judgments unsupervised
  • Teams that want a tool bought and installed with no change to how they work
  • Projects where nobody internally is willing to own the result
