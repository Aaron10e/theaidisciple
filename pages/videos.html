What these six categories are

The AI Disciple channel publishes six kinds of video, and each one exists to answer a different question. Together they are the working proof behind the consulting: 471 videos produced in nine months, in English and Urdu, by one person running an AI production pipeline.

Christian AI guide is practical teaching on using AI honestly and well — prompts, tools, and the places AI gets things wrong. Biblical AI research digs into archaeology, history and the text itself, showing the sources rather than asking you to take my word for it. Christian live-action stories are narrated Scripture films built for congregations and sermon use.

Christian testimonies are true accounts from real people, including families leaving the brick kilns in Pakistan. Christian story cartoons are the animated Bible stories for children, free to use in Sunday school — there is a dedicated library page for those. AI Disciple is the main category, where the newest and most general work lands first.

If you want video like this made for your church, business or nonprofit — or you want the pipeline built so your own team can run it — that is a service I offer.
